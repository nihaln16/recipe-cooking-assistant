Tomato Garlic Pasta
Serves 2

Ingredients
- 8 oz spaghetti
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 3 garlic cloves, sliced
- 1 cup crushed tomatoes
- salt

Steps
1. Boil spaghetti in salted water until al dente.
2. Warm olive oil and gently cook garlic until fragrant.
3. Stir in tomatoes, simmer 8 minutes, toss with pasta.
